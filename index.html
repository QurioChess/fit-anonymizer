What stays?

The goal is to retain workout data such as heart rate, power, cadence, speed, distance, elevation and timestamps. Some less common or manufacturer-specific fields may not survive re-encoding.

This prototype uses Garmin's JavaScript FIT SDK loaded from a public CDN. Your selected file is processed in this browser; it is not sent to an account or upload server. An internet connection is required to load the SDK.

Privacy note: this removes recognized FIT fields, not every possible fingerprint. Photos, exported filenames, unusual developer fields, or data added by a third-party platform may still reveal information. Check the result before sharing it.
